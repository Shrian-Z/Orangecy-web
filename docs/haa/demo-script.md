# 90-second demo script

Aim: prove the card loop and the music angle, not explain the whole company.

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

> "Most vocabulary apps show you a word and ask 'do you know it?'. People are bad at that question. Orangecy puts the word inside a song you already listen to, then brings it back when you're about to forget it."

Show the live demo home: `四级 200 词`, `学习 10`.

## 0:10–0:30 — Context card

- Tap 学习.
- Show the lyric/example line with the target highlighted.
- Tap the word: hear pronunciation.
- Tap the card: meaning appears.
- Point at the three buttons: 忘记 / 模糊 / 认识.
- Tap 忘记; show the same card comes back. Say: "Forgetting is a signal, not a failure; it retries in the same session."

## 0:30–0:50 — Recall + cloze

- Rate 认识; next card is recall (word + phonetic, meaning hidden).
- Rate 认识; next card is cloze (example blank).
- Say: "Same word, different retrieval direction. Context is not the test; it is the cue."

## 0:50–1:05 — Music layer

Cut to local full app (record separately):
- Lyric fragment with target highlight.
- Song audio plays 8–10 seconds.
- Pause; word pronunciation.
- Say: "In the full app, the cue is the user's own song lyric and the original audio, not a synthetic example."

## 1:05–1:20 — Why it matters

> "The hard part is not generating 10,000 definitions. It is choosing the right 3,000 words, writing the right example, and deciding what to test. I shipped the card loop and the 200-word slice first; the next step is the content pipeline and a delayed-recall metric."

## 1:20–1:30 — Ask

> "I want to build this with operators who have shipped learning products. Here is the live demo, the repo, and what I would do in the first month."
